### About

I am a Medior BI Developer at [Data Kingdom](https://datakingdom.nl) and a Master's student in Applied Data Science at Utrecht University. My professional and academic work spans business intelligence, machine learning, and applied research, with a current focus on healthcare analytics through my thesis at Amsterdam UMC.

I hold a Bachelor of Applied Science in ICT (Data Analyst and Scientist profile) from Fontys University of Applied Sciences and have over three years of professional experience across BI, data engineering, and data science roles.

### Current work

**MSc thesis at Amsterdam UMC.** Predicting post-operative complications following cancer surgery using machine learning and natural language processing on pre-operative physiotherapy assessments and clinical notes. Conducted under the supervision of Dr. Mihaela Mitici (Utrecht University) and Dr. Edwin Geleijn (Amsterdam UMC), with delivery from April through July 2026.

**ESG and financial reporting at Data Kingdom.** External BI Developer for CVC and DIF Capital Partners. Responsibilities include validating ESG and financial data across portfolio companies, performing data quality checks, and delivering automated, audit-ready Power BI dashboards for annual reporting.

**Coursework at Utrecht University.** Visual Analytics for Big Data, Personalisation for (Public) Media, Text and Media Analytics, Epidemiology and Medical Data Science, Data Wrangling, Data Analysis.
