### About

I am a Medior Data Engineer & BI Developer at [Data Kingdom](https://datakingdom.nl), building and maintaining data platforms on Microsoft Fabric alongside Power BI reporting solutions. I hold an MSc in Applied Data Science from Utrecht University (2025–2026), completed with a thesis at Amsterdam UMC.

I hold a Bachelor of Applied Science in ICT (Data Analyst and Scientist profile) from Fontys University of Applied Sciences and have over three years of professional experience across data engineering, business intelligence, and data science roles.

### Current work

**Data platform engineering at Data Kingdom.** Medior Data Engineer building and maintaining data platforms on Microsoft Fabric, supporting Jewa Group and CHDR with dashboarding and centralising their data landscape into scalable, Fabric-based reporting solutions.

**MSc thesis at Amsterdam UMC.** Predicted post-operative complications following cancer surgery using machine learning and natural language processing on pre-operative physiotherapy assessments and clinical notes, supervised by Dr. Mihaela Mitici (Utrecht University) and Dr. Edwin Geleijn (Amsterdam UMC).

**Coursework at Utrecht University.** Visual Analytics for Big Data, Personalisation for (Public) Media, Text and Media Analytics, Epidemiology and Medical Data Science, Data Wrangling, Data Analysis.
