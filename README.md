### About

I'm a Medior BI Developer at [Data Kingdom](https://datakingdom.nl) and a Master's student in Applied Data Science at Utrecht University. My work sits at the intersection of business intelligence, machine learning, and applied research, with recent focus on healthcare analytics through my thesis at Amsterdam UMC.

I was born in Crete, raised in Greece, and have lived in the Netherlands since 2019. I speak five languages, which has been more useful in stakeholder meetings than I expected.

### What I'm working on right now

🔬 **MSc thesis at Amsterdam UMC.** Predicting post-operative complications after cancer surgery using machine learning and NLP on pre-operative physiotherapy assessments and clinical notes. Supervised by Dr. Mihaela Mitici (UU) and Dr. Edwin Geleijn (Amsterdam UMC). Running April through July.

📊 **ESG and financial reporting at Data Kingdom.** Building Power BI dashboards for clients including CVC and DIF Capital Partners.

📚 **Coursework at Utrecht University.** Visual Analytics, Personalisation for Public Media, Text and Media Analytics, Epidemiology and Medical Data Science.
